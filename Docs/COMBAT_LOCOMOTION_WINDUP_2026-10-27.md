# Walking, stationary stance transitions and standard punch windup

Requested October 27, 2026. The player should walk with partial stick input, use the fighting stance only while stationary, blend the arms into locomotion and back, and load the standard punches naturally before extending.

## Changes

`RPGGASPInputBridgeComponent` now supplies the sample's `WantsToWalk` intent from the original Enhanced Input IA_Move magnitude. The world movement vector supplied by GASP is normalized, so measuring that vector incorrectly treated partial stick input as a full run request. Analog walking enters below 0.57 and continues until 0.73, around the configurable 0.65 threshold. Hysteresis prevents gait flicker. Existing keyboard walk intent is restored when analog walking ends. Full input runs, and existing sprint, strafe, crouch and flight controls remain available.

`RPGPrototypeAnimationComponent::UpdateCombatStance` fades the stance out over 0.30 seconds when ground movement starts. It no longer replaces the locomotion arms with a guard pose while walking/running. After movement stops, a 0.12-second settle prevents rapid toggling, then the stance blends in over 0.30 seconds while combat remains active. The eight-second combat timeout remains in effect; active blocking refreshes it while moving or standing. Explicit attacks and held blocks can still overlay locomotion. Standing punches retain body weight transfer, and moving punches retain the upper-body layer and running legs.

All three standard variants on both hands, including their first-person presentations, now have a compact reach-back: about 8 cm at the striking fist, a rearward elbow guide, an 8-degree distributed torso counter-turn and a protective opposite hand. The recorded punch then rolls out of this loaded pose into extension. The first 0.12 seconds supply the load; impact is at 0.36 seconds and recovery ends at 1.05 seconds. Montage blending starts from the currently evaluated pose, without playing a separate combat-stance preparation before the punch. Standard clips start/end on the latest wider stance for standing handoffs.

The normal punch's impact time moves with the animation to keep hit detection aligned. Hit radius, damage and the relative hit-window widths are unchanged. Captured charge entry now crossfades over 0.14 seconds to join the new reach-back to the existing charged load. RT/LT one-shot release behavior and independent hand/tier variant counters are preserved. First-person standard fists withdraw along the body strike path before blending back into their camera layout, avoiding a second forward motion into the target during recovery.

The strike-contact solver retains immediate push-out and its existing surfaces, compression, fold and reach limits. For a captured standard punch only, the existing correction unloads over at least 0.20 seconds instead of 0.06 seconds. This smooths small changes in pressure as the reacting head yields. Heavy punches, power punches and kicks retain their existing release time.

Only the 12 Light assets in `/Game/RPG/Animations/CapturedPunches` are reimported. All 24 Heavy/Power animation clips and their manifest entries are byte-for-byte/value-for-value unchanged. Stance assets, maps, project settings, get-ups and unrelated animation assets are unchanged in this pass.

## Authoring and verification

`RPG_SKEL=uefn python Tools/ProtoAnim/capture_punches8630.py` now defaults to a Light-only rebake that merges into the existing complete manifest. Set `RPG_PUNCH_TIERS=Light,Heavy,Power` explicitly for a complete authoring rebake. `standard_windup` supplies the compact shoulder/torso/arm load, and the donor motion still comes from IMG_8630. `captures/IMG8630_authoring_stance.json` freezes the original approved capture authoring basis, so adding the windup does not lower or otherwise refit the established strike path to the subsequently revised stance. The reach-back blends into that path before the hit window. `check_punches8630.py` checks all 36 clips, retaining each unchanged charged clip's original stance reference and requiring the refined standard clips to use the current stance.

Evidence is under `Saved/CombatLocomotion`.
