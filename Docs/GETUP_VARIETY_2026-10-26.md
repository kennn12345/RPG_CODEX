The existing110-degree segment-turn rejection remains (and the original best-available fallback when no start fits remains). Among safe near-equal choices it avoids the actor's `LastGetUpStyle`, then the last clip when appropriate. History is per actor, transient, and recorded only after a montage successfully starts. Safety takes precedence over variation.
- Fixed stale header wording referring to GASP's12. No GASP get-up is reinstated. The original14 assets, generator `getups.py`, original clip manifest, flight, combat/Power tuning, controls, maps and Physics Asset are unchanged.
